# VoLum 1.3.0 - PLAY

![VoLum PLAY in Dual Amp: amp art moving with the guitar](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/guitarlum/VoLum/v1.3.0/docs/volum-dual-amp.gif)

**Your amps, on stage.** VoLum now opens on PLAY: your Sounds on a pedalboard, with amp art that moves as you play.

### New

- **PLAY.** Eight stomp switches, a rail of your Sounds, and all 15 factory amps animated by your playing. Press **P** to jump into BUILD and tweak.
- **Switch Sounds from your foot controller.** MIDI Program Change (or a CC) recalls a complete Sound: amp, cab, pedals, everything. Standalone, VST3 and AU. Up to 128 Sounds in 16 banks of 8.
- **Plays out of the box.** Five Sounds ready on programs 0-4, and 21 factory presets across the amps.
- **Chorus.** A new pedal with four voices: CLASSIC, WARPED, CLEAR and ENSEMBLE.
- **Packs.** Back up or share your custom amps, IRs, pedals and presets as one `.volumpack` file.
- **Pedals you hear the moment you switch them on.** Pitch starts on the Octaver, Transpose at -2, Chorus on ENSEMBLE.
- **Tidier Settings** in three tabs, plus an optional once-a-day update reminder.

### Better

- The Octaver tracks the whole neck: no more wobble on high notes into a high-gain amp.
- Lighter on CPU and memory, especially with the window closed or several instances running.
- The standalone holds on to your audio interface and MIDI controller across restarts, even when it was unplugged at launch or you added new devices.
- DAW projects reopen on the Sound you saved, and Program Change now reaches the VST3 in REAPER.
- The portable build uses its own amps and pedals, even on a PC with an older VoLum installed.

### Get it

- **Windows:** `VoLum-v1.3.0-windows-setup.exe`, or `VoLum-v1.3.0-windows-portable.zip`.
- **macOS:** `VoLum-v1.3.0-macos-installer.dmg` (standalone, VST3 and AU). VoLum is not notarized yet: if macOS blocks it, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway**.

Your 1.2.x presets, custom amps and settings come along automatically. Going back to 1.2.x afterwards loses your PLAY board.
