07/02/2026 - VoLum (PRE Pitch): Fixed POLY stuttering/crackling at every non-zero shift. A previous change widened POLY's fixed-grain WSOLA search window to 24 ms - ABOVE the 20 ms grain band - which let the splice search jump more than a whole grain and retrigger a splice every crossfade (continuous crackle on real transient playing; inaudible on the synthetic sine/triad tests, which is how it slipped through). Reverted POLY's search + correlation window to the shipped 16 ms and added a hard clamp so the WSOLA search can never reach the grain band again, plus a unit test pinning the search < band invariant across sample rates. POLY latency, pitch and chord behavior are otherwise unchanged. (INSTANT, the low-string fix above, is unaffected.)
