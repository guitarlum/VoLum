# Lattice Reverence

A design philosophy for the VoLum collapsed PRE / POST blocks on the AMP-expanded view.

---

## The Movement

VoLum's existing visual language already speaks: charcoal voids cradle teal-and-gold filaments, corner accents whisper that every panel is a *frame* before it is a surface, and dormant elements receded behind a clipped diagonal hatch like blueprints stored in a drawer. The collapsed PRE and POST blocks have, until now, refused to participate. They stand as thin vertical totems with letter-stacks — monolithic, reverent, but mute. They tell the player *that* signal flows through them; they never tell *what* lives inside.

**Lattice Reverence** is the proposal that those flanking columns become miniature reliquaries. Each effect slot is a chamber: small, precisely framed, holding a single artifact — a fractal motif, a glyph, a pinpoint of light. The chamber does not shout. It is read closely, the way one reads a contact-print sheet: by approaching the surface, not by being shouted at from across the room.

## Form and Space

Form is reductive geometry: rectangles, hairlines, four-corner tick marks, a single circular LED. Nothing rounded for decoration's sake; rounding appears only where it serves grouping (a pill chip says "I am one item among peers"). Space is the principal material — gutters around each chamber should feel deliberately wide enough that a player's eye can rest between slots without crowding, narrow enough that the chambers read as a single ensemble rather than scattered notes. The ratio of inhabited pixel to negative pixel within a slot leans heavily toward emptiness; the artifact occupies the center third, the rest is breath.

## Color and Material

The palette is fixed and small: near-black (#0C0C12) substrate, the established teal (#5BC4C4) for active life, gold-cream (#FCDE91) reserved for the rare highest-priority cue (focused/selected), bone-cream (#EDE3D0) for body text, and a muted teal-dim (#4BA2A2) that is the resting state of any frame line. Active state is signaled by *return of saturation* — the dormant slot's hatch pattern lifts away, the LED ignites at full teal, the inner motif's alpha climbs from a quarter-strength ghost to its full chromatic weight. Bypass and dormancy are quoted from the existing diagonal hatch on PRE strips so the new elements feel native.

## Scale and Rhythm

Type scale stays disciplined: effect labels at 9-10 px equivalent in Josefin SemiBold caps with generous tracking; section labels (PRE / POST) one step larger and held at the gold-dim register so they recede behind the artifact. LEDs are smaller than instinct suggests — 3-4 px — because a pinpoint reads as a *signal*, while a coin reads as decoration. Rhythm across the row of slots is strictly modular: equal heights, equal gutters, the eye should feel a metronome when it scans the column.

## Composition and Visual Hierarchy

Hierarchy on the AMP-expanded view runs: amp art (loudest), amp name (second), knob row (third), PRE/POST chambers (fourth, quiet but legible), section labels and footer (faintest). The redesign must never compete with the amp art for primacy; its job is to *frame* that art the way a museum vitrine frames a sculpture — visible, supportive, never the subject. Each chamber's interior motif should echo the visual logic of the larger expanded effect's fractal art (compressor toroids, NAM lattice grids, delay decay-trains) but at a vocabulary so reduced that it functions almost as ideogram.

## Craft Standard

This is meticulous work. Each line is placed at integer pixel boundaries; each LED sits on the same baseline across its row; each label is letter-spaced as if set by a typographer who has held a metal forme in their hands. The result must read as the product of countless deliberate hours by someone at the absolute top of their craft — engineering grade, but warm. If a viewer cannot tell whether the chamber was rendered by code or set with a press, the work has succeeded. Every alignment the result of painstaking attention; every pixel earned through master-level execution.

## What This Forbids

No drop shadows. No gradients beyond the subtle radial bloom that already lives behind the hero art. No emoji or pictogram metaphors imported from generic UI kits. No animation in a static mockup. No font outside the established Josefin family. No introduction of a new accent hue. No element that breaks the existing four-corner tick frame language. The constraint is not poverty; it is the discipline that makes the few permitted gestures feel inevitable.

---

The chambers are quiet. They wait to be read. When a player engages, they ignite — one teal point at a time — and the signal chain becomes legible without ever raising its voice.
