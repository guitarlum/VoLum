# VoLum 1.2.0 — UI/UX IA convergence rationale

Scope: the integrated UI for F5 presets, F6 bring-your-own amp + Custom area,
F7 bring-your-own IR, F8 import your own pedals. All four ship together on
`feature/1.2.0`. This doc records the exploration of 8 information-architecture
directions and the converged winner that Phase 2 (C++ UI shells) implements.

The 8 directions were explored as a single clickable Cursor Canvas:
`~/.cursor/projects/c-dev-VoLum/canvases/volum-1.2.0-ia-directions.canvas.tsx`.
They share VoLum's locked Art Deco visual language and 900x600 frame; they
differ only in information architecture (where presets + custom content live and
how they are reached).

## Hard constraints (from the tickets)

- Default view stays uncrowded. Custom is a ~10% feature: "unobtrusive and
  secondary to the curated gallery" (F6).
- Do not structurally break the existing sidebar + PRE/AMP/POST triptych.
- Reuse proven patterns: the settings-overlay (`NAMSettingsPageControl` +
  `VoLumSettingsOverlay.h`, `HideAnimated`) for browsers/builders; the dropdown
  (`SetTargetAndDrawRECTs`) for menus.
- F5 specifies a compact preset bar in the AMP title strip with prev/next
  footswitch-style arrows for live preset switching, plus a full browser overlay.

## Scorecard

Heuristic: crowd and disrupt are lower-is-better; reuse is higher-is-better.
`score = reuse*2 - crowd - disrupt`.

| Dir | Custom entry        | Preset bar  | Crowd | Disrupt | Reuse | Score |
| --- | ------------------- | ----------- | ----: | ------: | ----: | ----: |
| D1  | sidebar section     | title strip |     2 |       1 |     4 |     5 |
| D2  | workshop mode       | header      |     1 |       4 |     2 |    -1 |
| D3  | sidebar inline      | title strip |     3 |       2 |     3 |     1 |
| D4  | locker drawer       | title strip |     1 |       2 |     2 |     1 |
| D5  | toolbar overlay hub | overlay tab |     1 |       1 |     5 |     8 |
| D6  | sidebar footer      | header bar  |     2 |       3 |     3 |     1 |
| D7  | bottom dock         | bottom dock |     3 |       3 |     2 |    -2 |
| D8  | sidebar section     | title strip |     2 |       1 |     5 |     7 |

## Verdict: D8 Hybrid (build this)

D5 (Custom Hub Overlay) wins the raw heuristic with maximum reuse and minimal
disruption, but it hides presets behind an overlay tab. That breaks F5's
explicit requirement for a compact in-context preset bar with prev/next
footswitch arrows for live preset switching while playing — the single most
important preset UX in the ticket. The heuristic does not capture this, so F5's
spec is the tiebreaker.

D8 keeps:

- the title-strip preset bar (F5 spec) with prev/next arrows + a dropdown that
  opens the full browser overlay;
- Custom as a collapsed `CUSTOM` section under the 15 factory amps in the
  sidebar — exactly where amps already live, satisfying the ~10% "unobtrusive"
  intent with zero new top-level modes;
- every builder / IR / pedal-import flow routed through the settings-overlay
  pattern (max reuse, no new modal paradigm);
- the IR loader discoverable in two natural places — inside the Custom area and
  as an inline card in the POST strip (where users expect a cab).

The one cost is the dual IR entry point, which must stay in sync (a single
shared IR state, two affordances).

## Runner-up and alternative

- Runner-up: D1 (Sidebar Custom Section) — identical to D8 but with a single IR
  entry inside the Custom area. The lowest-risk first cut if the dual POST IR
  card proves fiddly; D8 degrades to D1 cleanly by dropping the POST IR card.
- Alternative: D5 (Custom Hub Overlay) — only if live in-context preset
  switching is later deemed non-essential, in which case its higher reuse and
  pristine default view become attractive.

## What Phase 2 builds from D8

- Preset bar in the AMP title strip + preset browser overlay (Factory read-only
  vs User, Save / Save As / Rename / Delete, export/import, missing-content
  warning state).
- CUSTOM sidebar section -> Custom area overlay (amps / IRs / pedals).
- BYO-amp builder overlay: name, DIRECT + up to 3 autocomplete cab slots, upload
  -> assign to a sparse (speaker x channel) matrix with auto-snap and disabled
  empty slots.
- Custom amp selectable in the main view with sparse speaker availability.
- IR card (load / clear, on-off via `kIRToggle`, single optional level trim — no
  dry/wet mix; a cab IR is the cab, not a send) in Custom AND POST.
- Sidebar Custom is a single pinned bottom row (`Custom (n)`) that opens the
  Custom overlay; custom amps do NOT live in the factory list, so the 15-amp
  library never gains a scrollbar.
- BYO-amp builder is file-first: drop `.nam` files, each row gets a
  speaker + channel dropdown pre-filled from the filename, with a live coverage
  grid; manual assignment always overrides.
- Pedal import dialog (label + group) + merged PRE capture menu with stable
  indices.

Backend (persistence, serialization, DSP wiring) is deferred to a later session;
Phase 2 is pure UI shells on an in-memory mock model.
