# Oktaverb

Oktaverb keeps the reworked pitch-bloom idea, but staging repairs the sub-modes so they are distinct.

Visible controls:

- `Mix`, `Decay`, `Tone`, `Pre-Dly`, `Shimmer`.
- Sub-mode pill: `OCT`, `OCT+5TH`, `OCT+SUB`.

Mode behavior:

- `OCT`: octave-up shimmer voice.
- `OCT+5TH`: octave-up plus a fifth-up voice.
- `OCT+SUB`: octave-up plus a sub-octave support voice.

The pitch voice uses a pitched pre-delay and small per-line detune motion so the shimmer blooms after the reverb onset instead of sitting statically on top.
